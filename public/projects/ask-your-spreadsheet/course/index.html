Ask your spreadsheet

Asking ChatGPT or Claude about a spreadsheet can be a helpful first step if you’re new to data analysis. This short course adds seven small habits that improve the quality of the answers you get. You practice each one on a made-up member list.

Quick-start card

  1. Protect people. Remove names, emails and IDs, and only use an AI tool your organization allows.
  2. Shape the sheet. One header row, one row per record, no totals, one spelling per value.
  3. Write your definitions on a Definitions tab.
  4. Ask a sharp question: who counts, when, compared with what.
  5. Ask for the trace: what was counted, what was left out, where it came from.
  6. Check known answers first.
  7. Save the rules for next time.

Paste this before your question if you don't have the helper skill:

Before answering: check this spreadsheet for headers, totals rows, duplicates and inconsistent values, and tell me what you'll do about each. Use the Definitions tab exactly; if a word in my question isn't defined there, propose a definition and ask me. If there's a Checks tab, answer those questions first. Then answer with: the number, what was counted, what was left out, which sheet and columns, and how it was calculated. Don't repeat any personal details.
